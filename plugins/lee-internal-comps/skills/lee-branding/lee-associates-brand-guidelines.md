## Introduction

### About This Guide

Whether you are a member of one of our internal global communication teams or one of the
agencies tasked with helping us promote our brand, we appreciate your commitment to ensuring
that the Lee & Associates logo and brand is always reproduced in accordance with the usage
guidelines outlined in this document.

The Lee & Associates logo is a valuable brand and business asset. As such, it is our
responsibility to protect it and grow its value. Our logo should serve as the primary
identifying unit for all of our offices and Lee & Associates business. **It should appear at
least once in every communication.** When the Lee & Associates name appears outside of text,
it is to appear in the logo format. **It may never be re-drawn, distorted, or re-colored.**

### Core Company Statements

**Purpose**
To deliver a platform that gives brokers unlimited potential.

**Vision**
To establish an organization of brokers with unrivaled expertise in their local market.

**Mission**
To establish an organization of brokers with unrivaled expertise in their local market by
leveraging our revolutionary model; we will attract premier brokers and provide clients with
best-in-market expertise and service.

**Positioning Statement**
Lee & Associates is a full-service commercial real estate firm specializing in local market
expertise for building owners and tenants. Market by market, Lee & Associates brokers have
more years of experience, deeper knowledge of their community's dynamics and a vested
interest in every client's satisfaction. Through our nationwide network of local offices, we
deliver local insight, personal, responsive service and proven performance in every market we
serve.

**Tagline**

> **LOCAL EXPERTISE. INTERNATIONAL REACH. WORLD CLASS.**

The tagline directly aligns with the brand strategy and supports the greatest strength of the
Lee & Associates brand: their ability to provide unmatched local expertise. It is a promise
that Lee & Associates will continue to provide expert service and build the strong local
relationships that research insights determined the brand is best known for.

### Company Messaging

**The Lee Advantage.** Every Lee & Associates office delivers world-class service to an array
of regional, national, and international clients — from small businesses and local investors to
major corporate users and institutional investors. Our professionals combine the latest
technology, resources, and market intelligence with their experience, expertise, and
commitment to superior service to optimize client results.

**What Sets Us Apart?** Since 1979, Lee & Associates has reimagined the way commercial real
estate companies should be structured. Each Lee & Associates office is owned and operated by
its professionals. As shareholders of the company, this separates us from our competition and
creates one common goal: to provide seamless, consistent execution and value-driven
market-to-market services to our clients.

**Services to Meet the Needs of Our Clients.** Lee & Associates' offices offer a broad array
of real estate services tailored to each market — commercial real estate brokerage, integrated
services, and construction services. Specialty practice groups in each discipline collaborate
to provide clients with the most advanced, up-to-date market technology and information.

**Entrepreneurship & Equity.** One of our key values is ownership — real equity in the office
you participate in. Each broker has the opportunity to become a principal in their local
office. That principal/shareholder standing lets them share in the profits of their local
office and have a say in the national direction of the company. Culture at Lee & Associates
means the opportunity to be an owner of your local office, to participate in a profit pool, to
invest in the growth of the company, and to own real estate with partners or through the
Associates Equity Fund (AEF). Our shareholders are our revenue producers, and our goal is to
return those profits in reward for outstanding client service.

**Professional Development.** Our culture, driven by entrepreneurialism steeped in client
service, has always attracted the best and brightest. To ensure clients receive the highest
level of expertise, we believe continued education and training are key. In addition to the
annual Lee Summit conference, we host online and small-group training sessions for brokerage,
integrated services, marketing, IT, and administrative professionals. Lee & Associates is home
to the 5th largest number of Society of Industrial and Office Realtors (SIOR) members in the
world and ranks in the top 10 Certified Commercial Investment Member (CCIM) designee companies
in North America.

**Philanthropy.** CompassionateLEE, Lee & Associates' 501(c)(3) charitable organization, was
founded in 2019 and is focused on bringing people together to assist Lee family members and

## Chapter 1 — Logo Mark & Usage

### Master Brand Logo

The Lee & Associates master brand logo consists of **three elements**: the **Icon**, the
**Lettermark**, and the **Tagline**. **Never alter it in any way.**

The logo reflects an innovative and agile business where an entrepreneurial spirit thrives and
the focus remains on clients. It combines the business name (in a distinctive lettermark
style), a unique icon, and the descriptive tagline.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Icon** | A unique representation of the company monogram. The icon is **not** a substitute for the whole logo. |
| **Lettermark** | Should **never** be used on its own. It must always be accompanied by the Icon. |

### Clearspace & Minimum Size

- **Clearspace:** The logo should always be surrounded by a buffer of clear space separating it
  from text, other graphic elements, and the edge of the page. **Minimum clear space equals the
  height of the icon.** No other element may infringe on this space.
- **Minimum size:** The logo may not be reproduced smaller than **1.125 in / 28.5 mm wide** to
  ensure legibility.

### Primary and Secondary Variations

| Variation | When to use | Minimum width |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary logo** (with tagline) | Preferred version; use in all applicable digital and full-color printing. | 1.125 in / 28.5 mm |
| **Secondary logo** (no tagline) | Acceptable when the tagline will not reproduce well. | 1 in / 25.4 mm |
| **Vertical stack** | Only on approved collateral or digital mediums needing a vertical configuration. | 1.125 in / 28.5 mm |

### Color Variations

> **Icon gap note (applies to White, 1-color, and 2-color):** These variations have a gap built
> into the icon to show the separation of the letters in the absence of the gradient. This icon
> modification is **only** permitted with these versions — **never** on the full-color version.

- **White (inverted):** Use on the preferred Red background, or on a dark background/image. Do
  **not** place the white logo on bright off-brand colors or busy photographs.
- **2-Color:** Lee icon in **Bright Red (PMS 200)** + logo type in **Gray (PMS 424)**. Use when
  a two-color print restriction is in effect (e.g., apparel embroidery, promotional items, spot
  color). Not on bright off-brand colors or busy photographs.
- **2-Color Reversed White:** Use when the gray lettermark does not make the necessary impact.
  Same background restrictions.
- **1-Color:** For restricted/one-color applications, the logo appears in **100% black**. The
  only other one-color option is **Light Gray (50% black)**. No other tints or colors are
  permitted.

### Social Media Variations

- Standard social media logos are available in the **primary and secondary** color palette only.
  Accent colors and any other background are **not** permitted.
- Logos are sized with padding so they are not cut off on square **or** rounded platform image
  crops.
- The social media logo must **never** be used as a variation of the main Lee & Associates logo,
  and is only permitted as the **main profile image** of a Lee & Associates social media account.

### Sub-Branded Logos

- Permitted for **office, team, or specialty-group division** denotations **only**. They should
  not be used to create internal definition through names of departments, units, events, or
  clubs.
- All sub-brand logos not already in the **LeeHQ logos folder** must be approved by the
  **Corporate Director of Marketing & PR** before use on any internal or external collateral.

### Co-Branded Logos

In co-branding situations (conference sponsorship, alliance partnership, joint listing), the Lee
& Associates logo may be used alongside the partnering company. Always ensure our logo appears
**visually equal** to the partnering logo; adjust as needed to reflect that balance.

### Unacceptable Usage — DO NOT:

1. Alter the distance between elements.
2. Change the color of elements outside the provided logo color variations.
3. Stretch the logo.
4. Distort the logo.
5. Skew or angle the logo.
6. Retype or redraw the logo.
7. Outline or recolor the logo.
8. Use glows, drop shadows, or other effects.
9. Outline the logo to create contrast.
10. Use the icon separately in non-approved colors or effects.
11. Use the social media logo as a variation of the main logo. The social media logo must not be
    altered in any way other than the approved sub-branded variation.

## Chapter 2 — Brand Colors

### Primary Color Palette

Clean, bold, and distinguished — use for all brand applications.

> **Legibility note:** For text smaller than 10 pt, **Charcoal (PMS 425)** is recommended instead
> of **Slate (PMS 424)**.

| Color | PMS | RGB | CMYK | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Red** | 202 | 152, 0, 46 | 0, 100, 61, 43 | `#98002E` |
| **Slate** | 424 | 126, 128, 131 | 0, 0, 0, 61 | `#7E8083` |
| **Charcoal** | 425 | 48, 60, 66 | 43, 26, 23, 68 | `#303C42` |
| **White** | — | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | `#FFFFFF` |

### Secondary Color Palette

Used to enhance, in conjunction with the primary colors. Secondary colors should **never** be
used as the primary color of a brochure, social media graphic, or other document.

| Color | PMS | RGB | CMYK | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | 3035 | 0, 49, 70 | 100, 30, 19, 76 | `#003146` |
| **Sky** | 2925 | 0, 154, 218 | 85, 21, 0, 0 | `#009AD9` |
| **Frost** | 7542 | 169, 195, 203 | 24, 4, 8, 13 | `#A9C3CB` |

### Accent Colors

Use **sparingly** — infographics, charts, and diagrams.

| Color | PMS | RGB | CMYK | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Merlot** | 1817 | 78, 19, 30 | 30, 85, 59, 70 | `#4E131E` |
| **Bright Red** | 200 | 205, 20, 66 | 13, 100, 72, 3 | `#CD1442` |
| **Green** | 384 | 138, 148, 30 | 26, 4, 99, 35 | `#8A941E` |
| **Mint** | 7472 | 111, 201, 196 | 54, 0, 27, 0 | `#6FC9C4` |

**Accent pairing rules:**
- **Merlot** and **Bright Red** can be used together, along with all secondary-palette colors.
- **Green** and **Mint** should be used **only one at a time**, in conjunction with the secondary
  palette.
- Do **not** use a Merlot/Bright Red combination together with Green or Mint.

> Note: the source PDF lists Mint's CMYK cyan value as "654," which is out of range; transcribed
> here as **54** (the only sensible reading). Verify against the live asset before production use.

### Color Tints

- **Primary colors** are approved in tints of **80% / 60% / 40%**.
- **Secondary and accent colors** are approved in tints of **60% / 40% / 22%**, and likewise must
  never serve as the primary color of a document.

### Building Color Schemes (Bold & Muted)

Color schemes should be built using **3 colors** plus approved tints of those colors. One of the
darker colors should be used as the **anchor**, with the second and third colors complementing it.

| Scheme | Tints used |
|---|---|
| **Bold** | colors at **100%** and **40%** |
| **Muted** | colors at **60%** and **22%** |

- A primary color may be complemented by the secondary accents **Merlot** and **Bright Red**.
- Two secondary colors may be used together with an accent color for contrast — remembering Green
  and Mint are never used at the same time.

### Approved Gradient (Icon / Primary Gradient)

A **linear gradient at 145°**, used on the icon, with three color stops:

| Location | Color | PMS |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Bright Red | 200 |
| 65 | Red | 202 |
| 100 | Merlot | 1817 |

- **Icon gradient** may be used when bold, signature color is needed and it is not appropriate to
  use the cropped icon on its own.
- **Gradient photo overlay** — to create:
  1. Create a photo frame.
  2. Set the frame's background color to the Lee & Associates gradient.
  3. Drop in the photo.
  4. Double-click the image until you see an orange outline of the actual image.
  5. Set the image opacity down to **~25%** (adjust depending on the image).

## Chapter 3 — Typography

We use **two fonts** in our brand communications: **Avenir Next** (a geometric, architectural
sans serif) and **Minion Pro** (a contemporary, elegant serif).

### Primary and Secondary Fonts

- **Avenir Next** — primary font.
- **Minion Pro** — secondary; used as an accent, call-out, or headline. May be used as body copy
  on special reports or publications.

### Font Alternatives

- **Nunito Sans** — acceptable alternative to Avenir Next **only** when Avenir Next is unavailable.
- **Arial** (sans) and **Georgia** (serif) — standard Windows/Mac fonts; use in Microsoft products
  and web applications if Avenir Next is not available. (**Tahoma** is also listed among the
  web/Microsoft font family options.)

### Available weights (per the usage chart)

- **Avenir Next:** Regular, Medium, Demi-Bold, Bold
- **Nunito Sans:** Regular, Semi-Bold, Bold, Black
- **Minion Pro:** Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold
- **Arial / Tahoma / Georgia:** Regular, Bold

### Font files included in this package

As of **2026-06-24**, Lee provided a 3-weight Avenir Next family (the
*Cyrillic* cut), included in this package's `fonts/` folder (see that folder's
`README.md` for usage notes):

| File | Weight | Style | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AvenirNextCyr-Regular.woff` | Regular (400) | upright | WOFF (v1) |
| `AvenirNextCyr-Italic.woff` | Regular (400) | italic | WOFF (v1) |
| `AvenirNextCyr-Medium.woff` | Medium (450) | upright | WOFF (v1) |
| `AvenirNextCyr-MediumItalic.woff` | Medium (450) | italic | WOFF (v1) |
| `AvenirNextCyr-Bold.woff` | Bold (750) | upright | WOFF (v1) |

All cover Latin + Cyrillic, so English copy renders correctly. This is a
serviceable Regular/Medium/Bold deployment (body / emphasis / headings).
**Caveats:** it's the *Cyrillic* cut and the embedded family names split across
two families (`Avenir Next Cyr` / `Avenir Next Cyr Medium`), so each
`@font-face` must declare `font-family: 'Avenir Next'` + explicit weight/style;
files are woff, not woff2.

On **2026-06-25** Lee also sent four **Avenir Next LT Pro** Demi files in
**desktop OTF** (`-Demi`, `-DemiIt`, `-DemiCn`, `-DemiCnIt`). This is a
different cut that **Lee rarely uses** — kept as reference only, not part of any
render. The Cyr web set above is the cut Lee uses and is what we bundle.

**Still outstanding:** **Minion Pro** (accent serif).

## Chapter 4 — Brand Elements

### Double Curve

The curved element can be used with the gradient or in a solid color. The top-left and
bottom-right corners of the object should have a corner curve set (Illustrator/InDesign:
**Object > Corner Options**).

### Reversed Curve

Achieved using the Corner Curve element. When placing, make sure the underside of the curve
aligns with the edge of the page, allowing the red gradient to bleed off the page. **Use only in
the top-right corner of the page.** A white variation is available for colored backgrounds or
photo usage.

### Cropped Icon

The logo icon is **not** a replacement for the Lee & Associates logo and should never be used
without the full logo — the **only** exception being the back side of a business card or document
that carries the full logo on the front.

### Company Tagline

> **LOCAL EXPERTISE. INTERNATIONAL REACH. WORLD CLASS.**

Often paired with the URL **lee-associates.com**, mind trim and bleed lines when placing on
collateral.

### Textures & Color Overlays

Approved textures and color overlays are available as supporting brand elements (see PDF/LeeHQ
for the visual library).

## Chapter 5 — Assets & Resources

### Photography Standards

Photography style is a key differentiator for Lee & Associates in the marketplace.

- **Headshot guidelines** and **stock photo / architecture guidelines** apply.
- **Preferred architectural style:** soft, curved lines in the building or cityscape that indicate
